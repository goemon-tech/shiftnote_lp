Frequently asked questions

A few things you might want to know.

Will ShiftNote change my calendar events?

expand_more

No. Calendar integration is read-only. ShiftNote imports shift information to calculate your estimated pay without changing your events.

Can I add more than one job?

expand_more

Yes. Add multiple workplaces and manage their individual rates, closing dates, paydays and allowances together.

Can I use ShiftNote on my phone?

expand_more

Yes. ShiftNote works in the browser on your phone or computer.
