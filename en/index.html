Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

Q. Can I connect calendars other than Google Calendar?

ShiftNote is currently optimized for Google Calendar. You can also sync iCloud Calendar and Outlook through Google Calendar. Direct integrations with other scheduling apps, including TimeTree, are planned for the future.

Q. Can I customize night-shift pay and break rules?

Yes. Detailed settings include a 25% night premium from 22:00 to 05:00, automatic 45-minute breaks for shifts of six hours or more, 60-minute breaks for eight hours or more, and 15-minute time rounding. These options accommodate common workplace rules in Japan.

Q. Why is it completely free?

ShiftNote is currently in beta to help more people discover the service and better understand their finances across multiple jobs. Core personal features will remain free. Advanced tools, such as tax simulations and tax-filing integrations, are planned as optional paid features.

Q. How do you protect my calendar and personal data?

Data in transit and at rest is protected with TLS 1.3 and AES-256 encryption. We use Google’s official OAuth 2.0 authorization, so we never store your Google password. We do not share your data with third parties.
